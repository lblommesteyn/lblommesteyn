Constraint Scout

Which transmission facilities is a proposed generator, battery or large load likely to constrain? Ranked from public information only: queue history, published studies, market congestion, outages and HIFLD-style topology.

Research prototype. This is not an interconnection study and does not replace one. The model was trained and validated on a simulated ISO (a physics-based study procedure over a public 17,467-bus PJM-footprint case), because real study reports could not be fetched in the build environment. Probabilities are calibrated on that simulation only.

How to read it. P(constrained) is the calibrated probability that the ISO's study names the facility as overloaded with the project contributing at least 5% distribution factor. "±" is the spread across a bagged ensemble; wider spread and "no prior study at this POI" both mean weaker evidence. Expected constraints is the sum of probabilities over all candidate facilities; the cost bar shows probabilities over allocated-upgrade-cost buckets. Evidence chips are the features that pushed each facility up.

Limits. Far-field facilities the public-topology model cannot see, and facilities whose headroom depends on unpublished planning-case details, are the main sources of misses in the held-out evaluation. Real-data pipeline and full benchmark: see the repository report.
